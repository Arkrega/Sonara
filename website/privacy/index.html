TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

SONARA is built on a privacy-first foundation. This page explains what data the app accesses, what it stores locally, and which third-party services it communicates with — and only those.

Scope note. This policy is derived from the app's declared permissions, known open-source integrations, and documented behavior. Items marked [needs confirmation] have not yet been verified against the shipped binary by the maintainers. This is a working document and will be updated as the app matures. It is not legal advice.

This policy applies to the SONARA build distributed from this site and its GitHub repository.

1 — PERMISSIONS

Permissions requested by the app

Internet access Required

Streaming audio from YouTube Music, fetching lyrics, checking for updates, and authenticating a YouTube Music account.

Notifications Optional

Release notifications and optional event prompts. Can be disabled in system settings.

Network state Read-only

Detecting connectivity to choose stream quality and enable offline mode.

Boot completed System

Rescheduling music alarms after the device restarts (sleep timer functionality).

Wake lock System

Keeping playback running in the background while the screen is off.

Exact alarm System

Triggering alarm and timer actions at a precise time.

Foreground service Required

Keeping media playback and downloads alive as a persistent, controllable notification.

Microphone Optional

Music recognition feature ([needs confirmation]). Not required to use the player. The feature is declared but may be disabled or removed in shipped builds.

Install packages Optional

Enabling in-app updates ([needs confirmation]). Requested only when the update flow is active.

Modify audio settings Required

Enabling the equalizer, audio effects, and volume normalization.

2 — DATA COLLECTED

What data is stored locally

SONARA is a client application. It stores whatever it needs to play and organize your music — nothing more. By default, no data is sent to any server.

Metadata and library

Your library, playlists, ratings, and playback position are stored locally in the device database. These are your files and your organization.

Cached audio and artwork

Downloaded tracks and cover images are cached on device for offline playback. They can be cleared from settings at any time.

Playback preferences

Equalizer settings, playback speed, volume normalization, and other preferences are stored locally on the device.

Account credentials

When you log in with a YouTube Music account, credentials are handled through YouTube's own authentication flow ([needs confirmation]). SONARA stores only the tokens needed to act on your behalf.

3 — HOW DATA IS USED

Data usage

  • To play music. Fetching streams and lyrics is required for the core experience.
  • To keep your library in sync. If you sign in and enable synchronization, changes sync with your YouTube Music account through YouTube's services.
  • To keep the app current. Update checks connect to the official repository ([needs confirmation]) and are opt-in.
  • To show you relevant content. Recommendations and search results come from the music provider's own service.
4 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External services the app may contact

SONARA is a client of the services below. Data shared with them depends on your account state and which optional features you enable.

YouTube / YouTube Music

Audio streams, metadata, recommendations, and — when signed in — your library and account data.

Lyrics providers

Synchronized lyrics are fetched from multiple community sources ([needs confirmation]). Provider priority is user-controllable.

Google Cast

Casting audio to Chromecast and Cast-enabled devices requires Google's casting infrastructure when this feature is used.

Last.fm

Optional scrobbling. Opt in through settings and connect your account; then listening activity is shared with Last.fm.

Discord Rich Presence

Optional status sharing ([needs confirmation]). Requires a separate Discord authorization when enabled.

Backup storage

Library backups ([needs confirmation]) may be stored or transferred through the user's chosen cloud storage service when this feature is enabled.

5 — DATA STORAGE

Where your data lives

  • On-device only (default). All core data — library, downloads, preferences, EQ presets — resides on your device.
  • Cloud, by choice. Library backups, synchronized playlists, and account tokens use cloud services only when you opt in.
  • Ephemeral network traffic. Stream requests and lyrics lookups are transient and not retained by SONARA.
6 — SECURITY

Security and integrity

  • Integrity verification. When a production installer is published, it will ship with a SHA-256 checksum file so you can verify the binary matches the official build.
  • Open source. The source code is public and reviewable under GPL-3.0, allowing independent audit of network and data behavior.
  • No telemetry. SONARA does not include analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking ([needs confirmation] — to be verified against the shipped codebase).
  • Minimal attack surface. Only the permissions listed above are declared; no background data collection services are embedded.
7 — CONTACT & RIGHTS

Your rights and contact

Your rights

  • You can log in without using any optional features.
  • You can clear cached media and your library data at any time.
  • You can disable notifications, sync, and every optional feature.
  • You can export or delete your backups where supported.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Open an issue in the repository or contact the maintainers through the project's community channels.
