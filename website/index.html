GETTING STARTED

Three steps and you're listening

  1. 01

    Download the APK

    Grab the latest signed build from the download page. The SHA-256 checksum is published right beside it so you can verify the file.

  2. 02

    Allow the install

    Android asks once for permission to install apps from your browser. Approve it, then tap the downloaded file to install.

  3. 03

    Open and listen

    Pick a folder, let the library scan, and press play. No sign-up, no onboarding quiz, no permission begging.

New versions arrive as an in-app prompt: SONARA downloads the APK, verifies it, and hands it to the system installer without sending you to a browser.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is SONARA really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial, and no feature behind a paywall. The project is GPL-3.0, so it stays free.

Do I need an account?

No. You can listen completely anonymously. Signing in only matters if you want synced features that rely on an online account.

Where does the music come from?

From your own device. SONARA scans the audio files you already own; it does not bundle a catalogue or stream anything you did not add.

Why is the APK not on the Play Store?

SONARA is distributed directly by the developer. That keeps the release honest, the signature verifiable, and the project free of store fees.

How do updates work?

The app checks for a new release, shows a prompt at most once a day, and installs the APK itself. A “New Update” chip stays in the top bar until you update.

Is my listening data collected?

No. There is no analytics SDK, no crash telemetry, and no account tracking. Your library stays on your device.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. Clone the repository, run the Gradle build, and you get the same FOSS flavour that ships here.
