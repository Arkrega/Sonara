YOUR SOUND. YOUR WORLD.

A Premium Music Player
Built for Listening.

SONARA is an independent, ad-free Android music player powered by AndroidX Media3. Immersive playback, synchronized lyrics, a studio 10-band equalizer, and a liquid glass interface — all in a clean, open-source package.

MANIFESTO

We believe sound deserves a dedicated home.

In an age of algorithm-driven noise, SONARA exists for one purpose: to give you a focused, respectful space where music is front and center. No ads, no trackers, no distractions — just your library, your settings, your sound.

No Ads. No Distractions.

Playback is uninterrupted by banners, pop-ups, or video previews. Your music, uninterrupted.

Private by Default.

You can listen completely anonymously. Login is optional and never mandatory to enjoy the core experience.

Open & Auditable.

Fully open source under GPL-3.0. Every line is public, reviewable, and free.

FEATURES

Everything for the serious listener

Immersive Now Playing

Full-screen playback with large artwork, ambient backdrops, scrubbing, and gesture controls.

Synchronized Lyrics

Real-time, line-by-line lyrics from multiple providers, with timing and translation.

10-Band Equalizer

A professional parametric equalizer with preamp, presets, and custom profiles.

Audio Effects

Reverb, audio normalization, bass management, and headphone presets.

Speed & Pitch

Adjust playback from 0.5x to 2.0x and transpose pitch in semitones.

Music Library

Full library management with local playlists, imports, and sorting.
