CORE CAPABILITIES

Everything You Expect From a Flagship Player

Ad-Free YouTube Music

Access the vast library of tracks, remixes, live performances, and albums directly from YouTube Music with uninterrupted background playback.

Offline Downloads & Cache

Save single tracks, full albums, or entire custom playlists for offline listening with automatic smart caching of frequently played songs.

Synchronized Lyrics

Sing along with synchronized line-by-line lyrics from multiple providers, including real-time translation and word-by-word timing.

Studio Equalizer & Effects

Shape your sound with parametric equalization, bass boost, pitch adjustments, audio offload, and AutoEq headphone presets.

Private & Account-Optional

Listen completely anonymously without signing in, or log in with your account to synchronize personal playlists, likes, and subscriptions.

Secure In-App Updates

Receive notifications and install official stable updates directly from the Rega Music repository, with automatic SHA-256 integrity verification.

Release Notes:

Initial stable release of Rega Music — an independently branded, high-performance Android music player.

  • Independent application identity (com.arkrega.regamusic)
  • Ad-free YouTube Music playback via AndroidX Media3
  • Full synchronized lyrics and offline downloads
  • Studio-grade equalizer and audio normalization
  • Integrated secure update system with SHA-256 verification
