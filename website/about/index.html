WHO WE ARE

SONARA.

Your sound. Your world. An independent, open-source Android music player built for people who treat music as the thing, not the background.

THE PROJECT

What is SONARA?

SONARA is an independent fork and continuation of a YouTube Music player lineage. It combines a clean, stable playback engine built on AndroidX Media3 with a purpose-built user experience: synchronized lyrics, a 10-band parametric equalizer, advanced audio effects, and a custom glass interface.

The application identity is com.arkrega.regamusic, and the project is maintained in full public view. Everything — code, build configuration, and release notes — is accessible to anyone who wants to look.

VISION

Where SONARA is going

The vision is simple: a music player that respects the listener's attention and gives audiophiles genuine tools without complexity tax. In practice, that means:

  • Sound first. Studio-grade tone control (parametric EQ, reverb, normalization) available but never in the way.
  • Clarity over clutter. Editorial layouts, generous whitespace, and a monochrome identity that recedes so the album art and music take center stage.
  • Respect for privacy. Anonymous usage by default, optional features clearly separated, and no telemetry hidden in the settings.
  • Transparency by construction. Public code, public releases, public changelogs — so trust is earned, not declared.
DESIGN APPROACH

How we design

The interface follows the Material 3 foundation inherited from the upstream codebase, extended with a custom rendering layer. The result is a glass aesthetic that is coherent across screens: translucent surfaces, soft edges, and backdrops that breathe color from the album art.

Typography is the other pillar. A strong sans-serif stack for hierarchy and a monospace font for version strings, checksums, and developer-facing details. Every element is placed on an invisible grid, and motion is limited to what improves understanding.
