CAPABILITIES

Every tool a serious listener needs.

SONARA is built around eight core pillars — each one designed to put you in complete control of how your music sounds and how you experience it.

01 — PLAYBACK

Immersive Now Playing

The player is where you live. Large, artwork-led surfaces with smooth scrubbing, full-screen lyric integration, and a queue you can actually manage. Every interaction is timed — no lag, no jumps, just music.

  • ▸ Full-screen immersive surface with backdrop ambience
  • ▸ Queue view with reorder, remove, and search
  • ▸ Gesture-friendly controls and playback error recovery
02 — LYRICS

Synchronized Lyrics

Sing along with real-time, line-by-line lyrics. SONARA aggregates from multiple providers and presents them in a clean, glowing, animated display that follows the music exactly.

  • ▸ Line-accurate, scrolling, karaoke-style timing
  • ▸ Multiple provider sources with fallback
  • ▸ Glowing active-line effect and multiple animation styles
  • ▸ Optional AI translation and lyric export
03 — SOUND

10-Band Parametric Equalizer

Professional-grade tone control. Ten fully parametric bands spanning the full frequency spectrum, ±12 dB range per band, a master preamp with clipping protection, and your own custom EQ profiles.

  • ▸ Real-time frequency response graph with gain curves
  • ▸ EQ presets and editable custom profiles
  • ▸ Flat-reset and profile import/export
04 — SOUND

Advanced Audio Effects

Fine-tune the signal chain beyond the equalizer. Reverb, normalization, and calibration tools let you shape playback to your room, your headphones, and your preferences.

Reverb

A real-time convolution-style reverb engine with room-sized presets (small room, large hall, cathedral, plate, studio) and manual room-size, damping, wet/dry, width, and decay controls.

Normalization

ReplayGain-style volume leveling across tracks so sudden loudness jumps disappear and your sessions stay at a consistent volume.

Presets

Curve-based presets and per-song calibration let you swap signatures instantly — and save your own for repeat use.

05 — CONTROL

Playback Speed & Pitch Control

Slowing down a complex passage or speeding up a podcast — adjust playback from 0.5x to 2.0x. Pitch moves independently in semitones, or lock the two together in varispeed mode for DJ-style tuning.

  • ▸ Presets: 0.50x, 0.75x, 0.90x, 1.00x, 1.10x, 1.25x, 1.50x, 1.75x, 2.00x
  • ▸ Pitch transpose in semitones, independent of tempo
  • ▸ Varispeed mode links speed and pitch
06 — INTERFACE

Liquid Glass Interface

A custom rendering layer replaces generic Material surfaces with a purpose-built glass aesthetic — subtle translucency, soft edges, and a depth that follows your fingers as you drag through music. Minimal. Elegant. Yours.

  • ▸ Custom glass shader components across the app
  • ▸ Adaptive backdrop behind album artwork
  • ▸ Refined translucent controls and chips
  • ▸ Dynamic color palettes plus curated presets
07 — LIBRARY

Music Library

Your music, organized your way. Build local playlists, import from other apps, sync your YouTube Music library, and navigate artists, albums, playlists, and podcasts with a responsive, fast interface.

  • ▸ Full local-library management and playlist editing
  • ▸ Import playlists and reordering with drag and drop
  • ▸ Sync with YouTube Music account when logged in
08 — OUTPUT

Audio Output Selection

Choose exactly where your music goes — speaker, wired headphones, Bluetooth device, or a Cast destination. Switch between outputs on the fly and keep playback continuous.

  • ▸ Speaker, wired, Bluetooth, and casting destinations
  • ▸ One-tap output switching from the player
  • ▸ AutoEq-style headphone calibration support

Ready to listen?

All eight features are available in one clean, open-source app.
